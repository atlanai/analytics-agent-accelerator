Try these

  

    
Where did my money go?
  • How much of a Grumpy Moose floor ticket went on fees?
  • Is it cheaper to buy at the box office?
  • How much does it cost to print your own ticket?
  • How much does Gatewhistle actually keep from a ticket?

    
Scalpers and drama
  • Which show sold out fastest?
  • How many Grumpy Moose tickets did scalpers really get?
  • How many people actually turned up to the Grumpy Moose?
  • What was the biggest resale markup?
  • Did fans get their fees back when Velvet Thunder cancelled?
  • What happened to Kevin From Accounting's show?

    
Running the business
  • App, web or box office: how do fans like to buy?
  • Which venue is hardest to fill?
  • Did the rain keep fans away from Riverside Fields?
  • How many free tickets did we give away?
  • What would last quarter look like without the demand fee?
